## 2. Distinguishing Core Algorithm vs Experimental Setup vs Our Adaptation

To ensure academic honesty in our paper, we must clearly delineate the three tiers of IRCoT:

### Tier 1: The Central IRCoT Algorithm (Trivedi et al.)
The invariant scientific core of IRCoT is defined by three rules:
1. **Dynamic Interleaving:** Retrieval and Chain-of-Thought reasoning steps alternate cyclically rather than executing sequentially.
2. **CoT as Retrieval Query:** Intermediate reasoning sentences serve directly as search queries to gather supporting evidence for unresolved hops.
3. **Evidence Accumulation:** Newly discovered, non-duplicate passages are appended to an expanding context pool, which conditions subsequent CoT generation until a termination criterion is met.

*Verdict:* **Our implementation in `src/systems/ircot.py` strictly adheres to Tier 1.**

### Tier 2: The Original Paper's Experimental Implementation
The specific experimental setup in Trivedi et al. (ACL 2023):
- **Corpus:** Full Open-Domain Wikipedia (5.23 million passages).
- **Retriever:** BM25 via Elasticsearch server.
- **Model:** OpenAI Codex (`code-davinci-002`) / `Flan-T5-XXL`.
- **Prompting:** 6-shot to 8-shot in-context learning with hand-crafted multi-hop demonstrations.

*Verdict:* **Our project differs from Tier 2 in corpus scale, base retriever, model architecture, and few-shot formatting.**

### Tier 3: Our Controlled Baseline Adaptation
In our research test bed:
- **Corpus:** HotpotQA Distractor pool (10 candidate passages per question; 2 gold + 8 adversarial distractors).
- **Retriever:** SentenceTransformers `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` + FAISS `IndexFlatIP`.
- **Model:** Groq API (`openai/gpt-oss-20b`).
- **Prompting:** Zero-shot directive prompting (`STEP_SYSTEM`), holding the prompt architecture and model backbone identical to Standard RAG (System A) and E-RAG (System C).

### Scientific Consequence for Publication
We **must not** claim that our implementation is a literal replication of Trivedi et al.'s open-domain benchmark scores. Instead, we must describe System B as:
> *"An adaptation of the IRCoT interleaved retrieval algorithm (Trivedi et al., 2023), instantiated under our controlled distractor-retrieval test bed to provide a direct, methodologically fair comparator for iterative multi-hop retrieval."*
